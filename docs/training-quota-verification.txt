3. What I requested, why, and my fallback
================================================================

I did not file an increase request, because my applied quota is already 15
and the value I would have asked for is 2. Lab 4's pipeline runs one
ml.m5.large training job at a time; 2 covers that plus one overlapping job
(for example, a rerun started while an earlier job is still finishing), and
nothing in the lab needs more than that. Requesting 2 against an applied
value of 15 would be a decrease, which Service Quotas does not accept, and
asking for more than 15 would be capacity I cannot justify. The applied value
differs from the AWS default of 0 even though the request history is empty,
so AWS raised it on its own; this is the get-service-quota vs.
get-aws-default-service-quota gap the pre-lab warns about, and I checked both
rather than assuming. Because that applied value was granted without a request
from me, I will re-run the Step 4 check right before starting Lab 4 instead
of trusting this snapshot. If on-demand capacity is unavailable at that point,
my fallback is spot training on ml.m5.large (L-29688C85: default 4, applied 10
on my account), adding checkpointing and a retry to the training job since
spot instances can be interrupted; the job trains in under two minutes on
10,000 rows, so interruption risk is low. If I take that path I will note the
change in my Lab 4 submission.
